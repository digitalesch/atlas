# Atlas Manifesto

## Why Atlas Exists

Mechanical design is often tied directly to CAD.

This makes designs difficult to evolve, reuse, and compose.

Atlas separates engineering from geometry.

Instead of designing a single machine, Atlas defines reusable engineering modules that can be assembled into many different machines.

The compiler is responsible for transforming engineering intent into physical representations.

## Our Philosophy

Engineering should be:

- Modular
- Composable
- Deterministic
- Backend independent
- Open

CAD should be an implementation detail.

## Long-Term Vision

Atlas should be capable of generating:

- 3D Printers
- CNC Machines
- Laser Cutters
- Pick-and-Place Machines
- Robotics Platforms
- Custom Automation Systems

from the same engineering framework.

We are building a platform, not a product.

## Motto

> Design the engineering once. Generate the machine everywhere.
